SMS Terms & Conditions

Effective September 16, 2026 · Program: WCEMS Employee Scheduling Alerts

  1. Program description. WCEMS Employee Scheduling Alerts sends workforce scheduling text messages to Waller County EMS employees who opt in: open-shift page-outs, urgent staffing call-outs, schedule changes and announcements from supervisors, and decisions on the employee's own time-off, shift-trade, and shift-pickup requests. This is an internal employee program — it is not available to the public and contains no marketing content.
  2. Opt-in. You enroll by signing in to the employee portal at employee.wallercountyems.com, opening My schedule → My settings, enabling "Text message" notifications, and entering your mobile number. Participation is optional and is never a condition of employment.
  3. Message frequency varies with schedule activity. You can adjust which notification types you receive in the same settings screen.
  4. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan.
  5. Opt-out. Reply STOP to any message to cancel and you will receive no further program messages (a final confirmation message may be sent). You can also turn off "Text message" notifications in the employee portal at any time.
  6. Help. Reply HELP to any message, or contact office@wallercountyems.com.
  7. Carriers. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Do not rely on text messages as your sole notice of schedule obligations — the employee portal remains the system of record.
  8. Privacy. Information collected for this program is handled as described in the SMS Privacy Policy. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  9. Changes. WCEMS may update these terms; the current version is always posted at this address with its effective date.
