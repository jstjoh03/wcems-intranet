SMS Opt-In — Employee Scheduling Alerts

Carrier-review reference for the WCEMS Employee Scheduling Alerts program

Waller County EMS Employee Scheduling Alerts is an internal, employee-only text message program: recipients are Waller County EMS employees (~75 people) who individually opt in. It is never offered to the public and contains no marketing content.

The live opt-in form sits inside our password-protected employee portal (employee.wallercountyems.com → My schedule → My settings), so it cannot be crawled from the public site. This page reproduces the form and its consent language exactly as employees see it.

The checkbox is unchecked by default — consent is an affirmative action, is optional, and is never a condition of employment. The phone-number field sits on the same form, directly connected to the consent checkbox: ticking the box and saving gives that specific number permission to receive program texts.

Complete opt-in workflow

  1. The employee signs in to the employee portal with their Waller County EMS account.
  2. They open Scheduling → My schedule → My settings.
  3. They tick "Send me text messages about scheduling" (unchecked by default) — the disclosure above is displayed directly beneath the checkbox, with links to the SMS Terms and Privacy Policy.
  4. They enter or confirm their mobile number in the "Mobile number for text messages" field on the same form (prefilled from their employee roster record) — this is the number the consent applies to and the only number the program texts.
  5. They press Save settings. The consent (what was enabled, for which number, by whom, and when) is recorded in the scheduling system.

This web form is the only opt-in path — there is no keyword opt-in, no paper form, and no verbal enrollment. Employees can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message or by unticking the same box; HELP or office@wallercountyems.com for help.

Consent language, verbatim

Optional — never required. Frequency varies with schedule activity; message & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out (or untick this box), HELP for help. See the SMS Terms and Privacy Policy.

Messages sent under this program

Open-shift page-outs, urgent staffing call-outs, schedule changes and supervisor announcements, and decisions on the employee's own time-off, shift-trade, and shift-pickup requests. Message frequency varies with schedule activity; expected volume is well under 1,000 messages per month across the program.
