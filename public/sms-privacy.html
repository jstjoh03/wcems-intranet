SMS Privacy Policy

Effective September 16, 2026

This policy describes how Waller County EMS ("WCEMS," "we," "us") collects and uses information in connection with our employee scheduling text message program. This program is internal: text messages are sent only to WCEMS employees who individually opt in through the password-protected employee portal at employee.wallercountyems.com. We never send program messages to members of the public, and this program contains no marketing or promotional content.

Information we collect

  • Your name and employee roster record (already held by WCEMS as your employer).
  • The mobile phone number you enter when enabling text notifications.
  • Your notification preferences and a record of your consent (what you enabled and when).
  • Standard delivery metadata from our messaging provider (for example, whether a message was delivered).

How we use it

Your mobile number and preferences are used solely to deliver employee scheduling notifications you have opted into — open-shift page-outs, urgent staffing call-outs, schedule changes, and decisions on your own scheduling requests — and to honor opt-out requests. Consent to receive text messages is optional and is never a condition of employment.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, excluding the service providers we use to deliver text messages on our behalf (acting only on our instructions), and disclosures required by law. We do not sell personal information.

Opting out

You can stop receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message, or by turning off "Text message" notifications under My schedule → My settings in the employee portal. Reply HELP to any message, or email office@wallercountyems.com, for help.

Retention and security

Consent records and notification preferences are kept while you are employed and for as long as required for compliance and record-keeping. Access to this information is restricted to authorized WCEMS administrative staff and protected by authenticated, role-based access controls.
