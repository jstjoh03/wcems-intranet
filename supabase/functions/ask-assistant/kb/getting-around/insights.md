[Call Volume](/insights) shows the agency's monthly run numbers. Open it from the dashboard's Call Volume tile, the phone menu (☰ → Call Volume Insights), or search.

What's there:

- A **Month** picker (defaults to the latest month with data).
- Stat cards: Total Calls, Avg Response, In District, Out of District, Patients, Transports, UHU, Air Transports.
- Three tabs: **Trend** (monthly call-volume line), **By Unit** (runs, share, and average response per unit), and **By Zone** (a donut of scene zones).

The dashboard tile shows the current month's total with a sparkline and the change versus last month.

Data comes from the agency's monthly run report, entered by admins — if a month is missing or looks off, let an admin know. The trend chart needs at least two months of data before it draws.
