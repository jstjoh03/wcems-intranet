Announcements live in a carousel on the [dashboard](/) — there's no separate announcements page. The carousel rotates on its own every few seconds; use the arrows or dots to move manually (that pauses the auto-rotation), or the play/pause button. On phones, swipe between slides.

Reading:

- Longer posts show a snippet with **"Read the full story →"** — tap it for the full post (title, category, date, author, image, and full text).
- Tap an announcement's image to see it full-size.
- When comments are enabled on a post, the detail view has a comment thread — type in "Add a comment…" and post. (Shared kiosk logins can't comment.)

New announcements trigger a push notification — "{Category}: {Title}" — on every device that opted in (see the push notifications article). Only new posts notify; edits don't.

Posting, editing, archiving, and deleting announcements is admin-only — crew who want something announced should send it to an admin or the appropriate supervisor.
