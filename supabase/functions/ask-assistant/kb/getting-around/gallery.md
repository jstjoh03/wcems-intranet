The photo gallery at [Around the County](/gallery) collects agency photos — outreach events, stations, training, crews. Reach it from the desktop footer link "Around the County" or by searching "Photo Gallery" (Ctrl/Cmd+K).

Everyone can browse, filter by tag (All, WCEMS, Outreach, Stations, Training, Crew, plus custom tags), heart a photo, and comment on it — tap a photo to open it full-size with its caption and comment thread. Shared kiosk accounts can look but can't heart or comment.

**Uploading is admin-only.** Admins use the **Upload photo** button: choose a JPG/PNG/WebP up to 8 MB, add a caption (up to 80 characters) and a tag, then **Upload**. Admins can also delete photos; comment authors (or admins) can delete comments. There are no albums — tags are the only grouping.

If you have photos worth sharing, send them to an admin to post.
