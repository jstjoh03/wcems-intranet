The dashboard at [Home](/) is the landing page after sign-in.

Top to bottom on desktop: a navy hero (today's date, a greeting, which shift holds the county, and a count of upcoming classes), alert banners when something needs you (required training waiting, policies awaiting acknowledgement, or a push-notifications invite), a strip of featured shortcut tiles with a **Customize** pill, then Announcements, People (the employee Spotlight plus today's birthdays), and the Station Directory. The right rail has Your compliance, an MIH referral card, Upcoming Classes (with Register links), and a Call Volume tile that opens [Insights](/insights). The Training Library section at the bottom shows the three newest recordings.

On phones the same content stacks in one column, with slim shortcut strips near the top (Event equipment, MIH referral, and a compliance strip when you're caught up) and a floating **Quick Links** pill at the bottom of the screen.

Useful details:

- The hero's shift line ("X Shift · Day 1/2") comes from the live 48/96 rotation — see the shift rotation article.
- The Station Directory rows have a **Code** button that reveals a station's door code for 20 seconds, plus the address and phone when expanded.
- Birthdays show for teammates who left "Show my birthday on the dashboard" on; you can tap the heart to wish them happy birthday or leave a note.
- Navigation: on desktop use the navy nav bar (Home, Directory, Protocols, Training, Scheduling, Equipment, Policies, Hospitals, MIH Referral, Uniforms, Systems). On phones, the ☰ menu opens the same pages. Global search is **Ctrl/Cmd+K** anywhere — it finds pages, people, hospitals, stations, classes, and recordings.
