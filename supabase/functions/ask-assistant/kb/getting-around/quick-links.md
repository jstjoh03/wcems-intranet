Quick links are the catalog of outside systems (Outlook, ESO, Paycom, Supply Portal, Protocols shortcuts, and so on). They appear in three places:

- **Featured shortcuts strip** on the dashboard — your personal picks.
- **Quick Links pill** floating at the bottom on phones — the full catalog with search.
- **Systems menu** in the desktop nav bar (and the external-systems links in the top utility bar).

**Customize your featured strip:**

1. On the dashboard, tap **Customize** on the shortcuts strip.
2. Pick up to 4 links ("{n} / 4 selected"). **Schedule** and **Hospitals** always stay as the first and last tiles.
3. **Save**. (**Reset** returns you to the role defaults — for crew that's Outlook, Protocols, Employee Shoutout, and Supply Portal.)

**Pin links in the phone dock:**

1. Tap the **Quick Links** pill at the bottom of the dashboard.
2. Search or browse by category; tap the pin icon on any link.
3. Pinned links gather in a **Pinned** section at the top of the dock.

Pins and featured picks are personal to you. The catalog itself (which links exist, their names and categories) is managed by admins — if a system you use is missing, ask an admin to add it, or use "Report an issue"/"Suggest a link" in the page footer.
