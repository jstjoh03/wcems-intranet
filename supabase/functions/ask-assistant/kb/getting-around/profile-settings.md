Open your profile from the avatar in the top-right (phones: tap it, then **View profile**) or by clicking your name in the top utility bar on desktop. There's no Save button — each field saves as you change it.

What you can edit:

- **Photo** — tap the camera button (**Add photo** / **Change** / **Remove**); images up to 5 MB.
- **Phone** (under Contact).
- **Privacy** — two toggles: **"Show my birthday on the dashboard"** (your name appears in the People section on your birthday) and **"Include me in the Employee Directory"**.
- **Notifications** — push notifications for new announcements (see the notifications article).
- **Scheduling** (if you have schedule access) — text-message opt-in and mobile number, a per-alert-type Push/Email/Text grid, your shift highlight color, and unavailable days. These have their own **Save settings** button.

What you can't edit:

- **Station and Shift are automatic** — they come from the schedule's rotation. If your seat changes, they update on their own; part-timers without a rotation seat show no shift. Don't ask an admin to type these.
- **Birthday** is read-only — ask an admin to correct the date itself.
- Name, email, role, title, or fuel card: talk to an admin.

Your **fuel number** is in the avatar dropdown (phones): it shows masked as ••••••, tap it to reveal for 20 seconds, with a copy button while visible.

Sign-in is your @wallercountyems.com Microsoft account — the portal has no separate password to change. **Sign out** lives in the avatar dropdown, the profile footer, and the desktop utility bar.
