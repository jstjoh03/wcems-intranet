The [Employee Directory](/directory) lists colleagues' name, photo, title, station, shift, phone, and email. Find it in the nav (desktop: Directory; phones: ☰ → Employee Directory) or with Ctrl/Cmd+K search.

Using it:

1. Search by name, title, station, or email.
2. Narrow with the filter pills: Shift (All + each letter), Station, and Role.
3. Each card has a phone button (tap to call) and an email button — there's no separate detail page.

Only people who opted in are listed: the toggle is **"Include me in the Employee Directory"** in your profile (avatar → View profile → Privacy). Shared kiosk accounts never appear. If you just opted out, the list updates after the page reloads.

Fuel numbers and door codes are never shown in the directory.
