The portal can push notifications to your phone or computer for **new announcements** and, if you use the scheduling module, for **schedule alerts** (approvals, trades, reminders, time verification).

**Turn on announcement notifications:**

1. Watch for the "Stay in the loop" banner on the dashboard and tap **Turn on**, or
2. Open your profile (avatar → **View profile**) → **Notifications** → check **"Push notifications for new announcements"**.

On iPhone you must install the portal to your home screen first (Share → Add to Home Screen) — Safari tabs can't receive push. If the checkbox says "Blocked by your browser," enable notifications for the site in your browser settings, then come back.

Subscriptions are **per device and per browser** — turn it on separately on your phone and your computer. Untick the box to stop notifications on that device.

What announcement pushes look like: "{Category}: {Title}" with the first line of the post; tapping one opens the announcements section of the dashboard.

**Schedule alerts** are configured separately in your profile's **Scheduling** section (or in [Schedule](/schedule) → My schedule → My settings): a grid of Push / Email / Text per alert type — request decisions, schedule changes, open shifts & page-outs, trades, shift reminders, time verification, and scheduler announcements. Shift reminders default to 12 hours before your shift. Text messages require opting in and saving a mobile number; emails come from schedule@wallercountyems.com.
