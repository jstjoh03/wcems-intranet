Want to recognize a teammate? Send a **shoutout** — it's a short external form (Employee Shoutout on Jotform), not an in-portal page.

Ways to open the form:

- Dashboard → People section → Spotlight card → **"Send a shoutout →"**
- The **Employee Shoutout** tile on the featured shortcuts strip (a crew default)
- The Quick Links dock or Systems menu, under the shoutout link

The form asks for your name and email, the recipient's name and email, and the kudos text. Submissions land with admin staff automatically (they're stored and emailed to the review team) — nothing posts publicly on its own.

From there, an admin can turn a great write-up into the dashboard **Spotlight**: the featured recognition card in the People section, with the person's name, role, tenure, a blurb, and optionally a longer story and photo. One spotlight is active at a time, and its "Congratulations & comments" thread notifies the person being recognized.

So: submit shoutouts freely — the best ones become the Spotlight everyone sees on the [dashboard](/).
