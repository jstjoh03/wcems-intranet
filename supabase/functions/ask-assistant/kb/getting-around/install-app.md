The portal is a PWA — install it once and it opens full-screen from your home screen like a native app ("WCEMS Portal").

**iPhone / iPad (Safari):**

1. Open employee.wallercountyems.com in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** icon in the Safari toolbar.
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

**Android (Chrome):**

1. Open the site in Chrome.
2. A banner — "Install the WCEMS app" — appears a few seconds after load; tap **Install**. (If you dismissed it, use Chrome's menu → *Add to home screen*.)

A reminder banner appears on phones that haven't installed yet; the X dismisses it permanently on that device.

Two things only work well from the installed app:

- **Push notifications on iPhone** require the app to be installed to the home screen first — Safari tabs can't receive them.
- Full-screen, app-like layout with no browser bars.

**Updates are automatic.** The app checks for new versions every ~20 minutes and whenever you reopen it, then switches to the new version on your next navigation or the next time you open the app — there is no "update" button. If a page looks stale after a release, fully close the app and reopen it.
