FTEP (Field Training & Evaluation Program) is run from [/clinical/ftep](/clinical/ftep) by FTOs, supervisors, and clinical staff. Trainees interact with it through [My Progress](/clinical-development).

**For FTOs and supervisors:** the FTEP home groups trainees by track (new program, legacy, P2→P3 ride-up, AEMT upgrade). Each trainee's **Actions** menu starts a **Daily Observation Report** (one per training day, reviewed with the trainee before end of shift) or an **Individual Call Report** (one per evaluated call), shows their schedule/progress and what's still needed, and opens their message thread.

Writing a report: rate every category 1–5 or N.O.; anything below 3 needs a comment, and a 1 needs a documented situation. ICRs can be flagged to count toward the 10 required scored ALS call evaluations. Drafts autosave — **Save draft — finish later** — then **Review & sign**: the trainee reviews it and both sign on your device. If the trainee can't sign right then (asleep at shift change), tick the exception box and submit — they'll be prompted to review and sign later.

**For trainees:** your reports appear under **My FTEP reports** on [My Progress](/clinical-development), with a banner when one needs your signature — open it, read the PDF, and **Sign & save**. Program documents (guide, handbook, workbook, blank forms) are under **Program resources**, and the **Messages** thread reaches your FTO and the clinical team (posts notify everyone on the thread).

Some FTEP paperwork (certain evaluation forms) is still submitted via Jotform; clinical staff review those under Submissions and accept them into your file.
