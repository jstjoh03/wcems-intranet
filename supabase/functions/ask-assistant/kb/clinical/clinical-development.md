Clinical Development tracks every provider's credentialing — phases (NEOP Academy → Field Training Rotation → P1 Attendant → P2 In-Charge, with an optional P3 FTO track), certification cards, license dates, and field training.

What you see depends on your role (the nav entry under **Training** changes accordingly):

- **Most employees** get **My Progress** at [/clinical-development](/clinical-development): your current phase with a progress bar, your gates and compliance detail (read-only), assigned protocol exams, **My documents** (files clinical staff shared to you), **My FTEP reports** (with a banner when one needs your signature), **Messages** with the clinical team, program resources, any 2026 protocol skills still owed, and the Evaluate-my-FTO card.
- **Supervisors and FTOs** land on the FTEP section at [/clinical/ftep](/clinical/ftep) — see the FTEP article.
- **Clinical editors** (granted staff) get the full Clinical Development console at [/clinical](/clinical): Today (work queue), Board (compliance grid), People (employee files), FTEP, Submissions, and Exams.

If My Progress says "No pipeline record yet," your record appears automatically once you're on the roster — ask the Clinical Development Officer if it doesn't.

Questions about *why* a gate or phase shows a certain way go to the clinical staff — the assistant can explain where things are, but not individual credentialing decisions.
