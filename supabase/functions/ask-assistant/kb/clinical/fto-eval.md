At each phase transition (for example after passing a protocol test), trainees are asked to evaluate their Field Training Officer. You'll see a card on [My Progress](/clinical-development) — "Fill it out now" when one is owed — or you can open the [FTO evaluation](/clinical-development/fto-eval) directly any time.

The form:

1. The FTO being evaluated is filled in automatically.
2. Rate 8 areas from 1–5 (all required).
3. Four optional written questions — honest specifics help the program most.
4. Signature is **optional** — you may submit unsigned.
5. Tap **Submit to the Clinical Department**.

Where it goes: **only the Clinical Development staff can read it.** Supervisors and FTOs have no access to individual submissions, and feedback reaches FTOs in aggregate and without attribution wherever possible. (Submissions are tied to your record internally so the program can track that evaluations happened — they are not posted anywhere.)

If something needs attention faster than a form, talk to the Clinical Development Officer directly — see [Admin Staff](/admin-staff).
