There are two skills boards; the **Skills** card/button (on [Upcoming Classes](/training) and in the clinical nav) opens a chooser between them.

**NEOP Skills Day** ([/skills](/skills)) — new-employee academy check-offs. Evaluators (supervisors, admins, FTOs, and granted staff) pick the day, a station, and a candidate, then mark every criterion Pass or Redo (a Redo needs a remediation note, and some actions auto-trigger remediation). When everything's marked: review the result, capture **both signatures** — trainee and evaluator — and **Submit check-off**. Any Redo makes the whole station a remediation; the second attempt revisits just those items with fresh signatures. The live board shows every trainee × station, builds signed packet PDFs, and runs the second-attempt queue. Candidates see their own results; passing every station checks their NEOP gate automatically.

**2026 Protocol Updates** ([/skills/protocol-updates](/skills/protocol-updates)) — the campaign verifying the new-protocol skills for **every credentialed employee, by level** (EMT, Advanced EMT, Paramedic, Licensed Paramedic). Everyone sees "My skills" with each skill's status: verified, pending, or remediation. Evaluators additionally get the full board by level — tap a pending chip to run the check-off, which is laid out like the paper form: check the criteria, mark VERIFIED or REMEDIATION, and capture employee + evaluator signatures. Completed forms can be viewed, re-verified, or exported (blank PDFs and a CSV).

To get your protocol-update skills signed off, catch an evaluator on shift. Remember: completing the protocol-update *video* in [Required Training](/training/required) is a separate requirement from the hands-on skill verification.
