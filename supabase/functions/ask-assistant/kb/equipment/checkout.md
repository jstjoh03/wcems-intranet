[Equipment](/equipment) tracks event gear chain-of-custody: what's on the shelf, out at events, in transit, or reported not found. Anyone can view the board, look up an item by its PSTrax tag ("Search tag, name, or type…" or /equipment/item/TAG), and see custody history.

**Checking out** is for equipment handlers (supervisors, admins, and granted staff): **Check out equipment** → pick items → say what it's for, which truck (by truck number), and when. A multi-night deployment uses the **Extended assignment** toggle with From/Through dates. Delivery hand-offs capture the receiver's signature on your phone.

**What regular crews do:**

- **Confirm it's here** when gear arrives, and run **Start-of-shift** / **End-of-shift checks** on extended assignments. Every item starts as "here" — tap anything that isn't to mark it Not found (a note saying what's missing and where you looked is required).
- **The end-of-shift check requires a photo** of where you're leaving the gear. Start-of-shift doesn't.
- **Close out the event** when it's truly over — tick "The event is over — this equipment won't be needed again." If another crew works it tomorrow, don't close; they'll check the gear when they come on. Closed too soon? **Reopen the event** undoes it.
- A **Print card** PDF travels with the gear (QR code + item checklist), and **Send to crew** shares the link.

Missing or damaged gear: mark it Not found during a check with a note, and tell your supervisor right away. Handlers record pickups, drop-offs at Admin, and write-offs.

You'll get a push reminder — "Event equipment check" — when a scheduled event assignment starts. Shared kiosk logins can view but can't record custody.
