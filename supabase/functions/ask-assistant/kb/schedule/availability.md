title: Marking a day unavailable
audience: all
route: /schedule
---

If you just need the schedulers to know you can't work a certain day — no time-off request, no balance involved — mark it unavailable:

1. Open [Schedule](/schedule) → **Requests** → **New request** → **Time off**.
2. Choose **"Mark a day off unavailable"**.
3. Pick the date, optionally add a reason, and tap **Mark unavailable**.

It saves instantly — no approval needed. If a scheduler later tries to assign you that day, they get a warning first.

Your marked days are listed right there under "Your unavailable days" (each with a **Remove** button) and in **My schedule → My settings → Unavailable days**.

That's the whole availability feature: there's no separate "available to work" signup. If you're part-time and want shifts, watch for open seats and page-outs instead — see the extra hours and open shifts article.
