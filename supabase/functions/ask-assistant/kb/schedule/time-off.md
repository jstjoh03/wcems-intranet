Two ways to start, both in [Schedule](/schedule):

**From the Requests tab:**

1. Open **Requests** → **New request** → **Time off**.
2. Pick a type: Vacation, Sick, Unpaid time off, Bereavement, Deployment, or Other.
3. Either pick days under "From my scheduled shifts" (your next 45 days, with "Edit time" for partial days) or use "Custom date" with From/Until times.
4. Add comments if needed and **Submit request**.

**From a board:** tap your own shift → **Request time off** → type, window, comments → **Submit time-off request**.

What happens next: the request goes to the schedulers ("the Chief") for approval. You'll get a push + email when it's approved or denied. Once approved, your seat shows OPEN on the boards for that window so it can be picked up.

Rules worth knowing:

- **Vacation and Sick are balance-checked** — if the hours requested exceed what you'll have (counting accruals through the last day and anything already pending), the app blocks it and suggests shortening it or using Unpaid time off.
- **Cancel while pending** from My schedule → "Your pending requests" → Cancel (or tap the Pending row on a board). After approval, only a scheduler can change or remove it — ask them.
- Balances show at the top of **My schedule** for full-time employees (hours, with accrual per pay period based on tenure). **View details** opens the ledger and planner.
- To just warn schedulers you can't work a day (no time off needed), use "Mark a day off unavailable" — see the availability article.
