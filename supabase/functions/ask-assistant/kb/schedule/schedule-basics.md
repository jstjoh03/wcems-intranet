Open [Schedule](/schedule) from the nav ("Scheduling"). On desktop it runs as its own app with a navy bar — use **Employee Portal** on the left to come back. Phones get a swipeable tab strip.

**Boards** (Schedule menu): Month, Day, Week, and Pay period. Desktop opens on Month; phones open on Day. Move with Previous / **Today** / Next or the jump-to-date picker.

**Who's working today:** the Day board. The header names the on-duty shift and counts open seats; below, each station's units list their seats — name, credential, and start–end times, with "R" marking a regular rotation seat. Open seats are tappable (see the extra hours article for pickups). Boxes below the roster show Extra Hours, Trades, Time Off, Special events, and Pending Requests for that day.

**Your own shifts:** the **My schedule** tab — the same views filtered to you (your days highlighted in your chosen color), a "N shift days this month" count, your pending requests, and the **My settings** button (notifications, text opt-in, shift highlight, unavailable days). Full-timers also see leave balances at the top: Vacation and Sick hours with the next accrual date, and **View details** opens the full ledger plus a "How much will I have on [date]" planner.

A gold strip on the boards reminds you when shift-time confirmations or a pay-period sign-off are waiting — see the time verification article.

If the page says "Scheduling isn't open yet," your account hasn't been given schedule access — ask an admin.
