Trades live on the **Trades** tab of [Schedule](/schedule). Two kinds:

- **Give away** — anyone qualified can claim your shift.
- **Swap** — you want a shift back in return.

**Posting:**

1. Trades → **Post a shift** (or tap your shift on a board → "Post as a trade (swap)" / "Give this shift away").
2. Pick the type, your shift (next 120 days), and where it goes: **the trade board** (anyone can respond) or **a specific person**.
3. Optionally trade part of the shift (From/Until), add comments, then **Post to the board** / **Send the request**.

**Responding:**

- Giveaway: tap **Claim** — first claim wins and goes straight to the Chief for approval.
- Swap: tap **Offer a shift**, pick one of yours, **Send offer**. The poster reviews offers and accepts or declines; an accepted deal then goes to the Chief.

**Nothing changes the calendar until a scheduler approves it.** Agreed deals show "Awaiting Chief approval"; after approval both calendars rewrite automatically and both members get a push + email.

Rules the app enforces: trades close at shift start; you must be qualified for the seat (both directions on a swap); one offer per person per posting; event assignments can only be given away whole. You can withdraw a posting or an offer any time before it's accepted. The Trades tab badge counts items waiting on you.
