Payroll runs on verified times, in three layers. You'll get push + email prompts (never texts) and a gold reminder strip on the [Schedule](/schedule) boards; there's also a pop-up when something's waiting.

**1. After each shift — confirm your times.** Open My schedule → "Verify your shift times." For each day you can tap:

- **My times are right** — done.
- **I worked extra** — opens the extra-hours form.
- **I left early / time off** — opens the time-off form.
- **Something else is off** — type a note and **Send to the schedulers** (files a time discrepancy for them to fix).

**2. Supervisors attest the trucks.** The morning after, on-duty supervisors confirm each truck's roster matched reality ("Roster matched" or flag it with a note). Crew don't do anything here.

**3. Pay-period sign-off.** The Sunday after a period closes you'll be prompted ("Approve your pay-period hours", with a last call at 9:00 — **due by 10:00 that morning**; HR keys Paycom Monday). Review the day table and either **Approve — my times are right** or **Something's wrong** (pick the day, add a note, send). Once signed off, you can't change it yourself — contact a scheduler.

Pay periods run Sunday–Saturday (two weeks); payday is the Friday after the period ends. Questions about a specific discrepancy go to the schedulers via the "Something's wrong" flow, which files it directly in their queue.
