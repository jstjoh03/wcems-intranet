**Claim an open seat:** open seats show on every board of [Schedule](/schedule) (the Day board header counts them). Tap one → **Request this shift**. Schedulers approve pickups first-come-first-listed; you must hold the seat's required credential (if not, a scheduler can still assign you directly).

**Request extra hours you worked or will work:**

1. **Requests** → **New request** → **Extra hours**.
2. Fill in the window (Starts/From/Ends/Until), Unit, Position, and a time type — Regular, Instructor, Meeting, Special Event, or Deployment.
3. Add comments and submit; a scheduler approves it onto that day's **Extra Hours** box.

Long windows trigger warnings (and anything over 48 hours is rejected).

**Special events** (standbys, games, fair coverage): schedulers post them with Paramedic/Attendant seats. Open event rows show on the Day board — claim them like open shifts. If you're assigned directly, you'll get a "You were assigned" notification, and an equipment-check reminder fires when the event starts.

**Page-outs:** when supervisors need coverage fast, they send a page-out (push/email/text) that can include the open shifts — tap a shift in the message to open the pickup flow. Read past ones under the **Messages** tab. Make sure your notification settings ("Open shifts & page-outs") are on if you want these — see your profile's Scheduling section.
