[Hospitals](/hospitals) is the receiving-facility directory — 20+ hospitals from the Texas Medical Center out to Bryan/College Station and Brenham, sorted by trauma level by default.

Finding the right facility:

1. Search by name, address, or notes.
2. Filter with the chips: trauma level (All, Trauma I–IV), plus Stroke: Comprehensive, Stroke: Primary, PCI Capable, Pediatric, and Maternal.
3. The Sort menu offers Trauma level (default), Name, Stroke level, or PCI capable first.

Tap a hospital to expand it: capability chips (stroke, PCI, maternal, NICU, pediatric), the address (opens your map app), notes, and the door codes.

**Door codes:** each hospital's **ER door** and **EMS room** codes have a **Reveal** button — the code shows for 20 seconds with a draining bar, then hides itself (tap again to hide sooner). Some facilities are marked "Ring bell" instead of having a code. The assistant never repeats codes — always read them from the hospital card.

**If a code changed:** anyone signed in can fix it — tap the pencil next to the code, type the new one, and save. Every change is logged with your name and the time, and the card shows "Updated by … · when".

Hospital details themselves (names, capabilities, addresses) are managed by admins at [Manage Hospitals](/admin/hospitals).
