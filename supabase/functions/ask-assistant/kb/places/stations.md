Station info lives in the **Station Directory** section of the [dashboard](/) — there's no separate stations page. Jump to it from search (Ctrl/Cmd+K → "Station Directory" or a station's name) or the phone menu's "On this page → Stations" while on the dashboard.

Each station row shows:

- A **Code** button that reveals the door code for 20 seconds (a draining bar shows the time; tap again to hide early). Stations marked **App Access** unlock another way and have no code to reveal.
- Expand the row for the address (opens your map app) and phone (tap to call).

**If a door code changed:** tap **Update code** on the expanded row, type the new code, and save — any signed-in user can do this, and every change is stamped with your name and time and kept in an audit log.

The assistant never repeats door codes — read them from the station row.

Your own station assignment (shown in your profile) isn't set here — it comes automatically from your rotation seat in the schedule. Station records themselves (addresses, phones, names) are managed by admins at [Manage Stations](/admin/stations).
