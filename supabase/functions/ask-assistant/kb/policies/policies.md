[Policies](/policies) lists the clinical, operational, HR, and general policies you're required to read and sign. Outstanding ones are flagged by a dashboard banner ("Policies awaiting acknowledgement") and the desktop compliance card.

**To acknowledge a policy:**

1. Open [Policies](/policies) and pick one under "awaiting acknowledgement".
2. Read the PDF in the viewer — the progress bar tracks you ("Reading X / Y — scroll to the end to enable acknowledgement").
3. When you've scrolled to the end, the three attestation checkboxes unlock:
   - "I have read this policy in full."
   - "I understand its contents."
   - "I know where to find this policy on the intranet."
4. Check all three, sign the signature pad, and tap **Acknowledge and submit**.

A signed certificate PDF downloads automatically, and you can re-download it from the policy page any time. The acknowledgement records your signature, the time, and the policy version.

**Why a policy came back after you signed it:** acknowledgements are tied to a policy *version*. When an admin uploads a new revision, the version bumps and everyone must re-acknowledge — the row shows "Update — please re-acknowledge." Your old signature is kept for audit; it just doesn't cover the new text.

Notes: station kiosk accounts can't acknowledge (sign in with your own account), and which policies you see depends on the audience the admin set (role, shift, employment type) — "No policies assigned to you yet" means none currently target you.
