[Kudos](/admin/kudos) is the inbox for Employee Shoutout submissions (the external Jotform). Each card shows who it's for, who sent it, the date, and "Read the full kudos" — submissions also arrive by email to the review team.

Two actions per submission:

- **Turn into spotlight** — opens the Spotlight editor prefilled with the recipient and the write-up, ready to polish: name, role/title, tenure, a short blurb, an optional full story and photo, then **Publish**. The Spotlight is the recognition card in the dashboard's People section; publishing replaces the current one (only one is active at a time), and its congratulations thread notifies the person recognized.
- **Delete** — tap twice to confirm.

There's no approve/decline queue — nothing posts publicly unless you make it the Spotlight.
