[Manage Call Volume](/admin/call-volume) feeds the [Insights](/insights) page and the dashboard tile.

**Automatic:** a daily sync reads the monthly run-report PDF from the designated "Call Volume" mail folder and loads the month — including replacing a month when a corrected report is emailed. Most months need no manual work.

**Manual entry or corrections:** **Add a month** (defaults to the previous month) with department totals (total incidents, average response), EMS breakdowns (in/out of district, patients, transports, UHU, air transports), per-unit run counts (Medic units, Supervisor, Community Paramedic), and per-zone calls. Saving over an existing month replaces it — the form warns you. The report month itself can't be edited after saving; delete the month and re-add it.

Crews see the data as soon as it saves. The trend chart needs at least two months before it draws.
