Credentialing is run from the [Clinical Development console](/clinical) by **clinical editors** — a separate grant list from portal admin (managed via the Editors panel on the legacy board). The phase ladder: NEOP Academy → Field Training Rotation (FTR) → P1 Attendant → P2 In-Charge, plus the opt-in P3 FTO ride-up track and an AEMT upgrade track.

Day to day: **Today** is the work queue (field-training queue, expiring/expired credentials, classes to schedule, Action Center), **Board** is the all-member compliance grid with CSV export, and **People** opens each employee file (licensure, cards, gates, field training, exams, megacodes, documents, messages).

**Advancing someone:** on the trainee's card, **"Advance to <next phase>"** opens the Phase Advancement Record — verify the four certifications, document a waiver if the phase is under its day minimum, set the next phase's start date and FTO, capture the **employee's signature**, and **Sign as CDO & advance**. The record PDF files to their documents automatically, and an FTO change triggers the trainee's FTO evaluation. On the employee file, Actions also offers program-track enrollment, **Promote — clear <phase>**, full record editing, and clinical-tracking exclusion.

The **accelerated P2 pathway** (10-day standard vs the traditional 36) is a checkbox in Edit full record; the card then shows "P1 → P2 · accelerated".

Advancement ripples outward automatically: the scheduler's credential derivation follows the phase immediately, and the person's GAL job title updates on the next daily sync.
