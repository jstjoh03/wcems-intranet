[Usage](/admin/usage) shows how the agency actually uses the portal, from page views logged on every route change (times in Central).

Panels: roster size and "ever signed in" reach; active users over 30 days / 7 days / 24 hours; views this week vs last; people-per-day and page-views-per-day charts (30 days); time-of-day pattern with the peak hour; engagement depth (power users with 15+ active days, regulars, occasional, dormant — with names — and never signed in); "where the time goes" by section; top pages; most engaged people; and a per-person drill-down of pages used and daily activity.

Use it to spot who never adopted the portal (the dormant and never-signed-in lists) and which features carry the traffic. Tracking is route-level only — it doesn't record individual clicks or form activity.
