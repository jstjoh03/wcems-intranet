[Manage Policies](/admin/policies) publishes policies and tracks acknowledgements.

**New policy:** title, category (Clinical / Operational / HR / General), summary, effective date, review cycle (Annual / Biennial / As needed — informational, nothing auto-re-prompts), an audience (role/shift/employment chips; empty = everyone), the attestation statement, and Active. After saving, attach the document with **Upload PDF** on the row.

**Versioning is the key mechanic:** the first upload is v1. **Replace PDF** bumps the version, keeps every older file and every old acknowledgement for audit, and flips everyone back to outstanding — crews see "Update — please re-acknowledge" and the dashboard banner returns. Editing the title, summary, audience, or attestation does **not** bump the version — only a new PDF does.

**The roster** (per policy): acknowledged %, stale and outstanding counts, per-person status chips (Signed / Admin-marked / Stale (vN) / Not signed), the same per-person require/exclude override as required training, **Mark acknowledged** with a note ("Signed paper copy on file"), and PDF/CSV exports — the PDF includes signature images; stale counts as not signed.

Crews read and sign at [Policies](/policies): scroll-to-end gate, three attestation checkboxes, signature, auto-downloaded certificate. Deleting a policy removes all its acknowledgements — archive by unticking Active instead when in doubt.
