The [Training Library](/training/recordings) holds past training videos — protocol updates, Doc Day recordings, skills refreshers, and more.

Finding something:

1. Search by title, instructor, topic, or tag.
2. Or browse: category chips (Doc Day, Protocol Updates, Skills, Community Paramedicine, Operations, Equipment, Annual / Required…) with a "Recently Added" strip of the newest uploads.
3. Sort by Newest, Oldest, Most viewed, or A→Z.

Tap a recording to play it right in the portal — YouTube, SharePoint, and direct videos all play in place. Items tagged **Required** are cross-listed required-training modules: tapping one takes you to its module page (with the attestation flow) instead of a plain player.

Recordings are posted by admins via [Manage Training Library](/admin/training-recordings). If a class you missed isn't in the library, ask the training team whether a recording exists.
