[Upcoming Classes](/training) lists every scheduled class — AHA card classes, CE lectures, and calendar events — grouped by month, synced automatically every 15 minutes from Wix Bookings and the education calendar. Rows show the date, time, location, instructor, and for seat-limited classes a "Registered X / Y" count.

**Registering:**

- **AHA card classes** (CPR/ACLS/PALS): tap **Register** — it opens the Internal Education page on wallercountyems.com, where you book through the website. Use your account there to manage or cancel the booking.
- **CE lectures**: tap **Register** — a short form asks your name, work email, certification level, and whether you'll attend in person or virtually (Teams). Your CE certificate is emailed to the work email you enter.
- Calendar-only events have no registration — just show up.

**Cancelling:** there's no cancel button in the portal. For a card class, cancel the booking on the website where you registered; for a lecture, contact the training team (see [Admin Staff](/admin-staff)) so your seat opens up.

**On class day** you'll usually scan a QR code to check in; card classes also collect your eCard email and mailing details. After class, the evaluation (and quiz, if the lecture has one) are required before CE credit is awarded.

The **Skills** card on this page (shown to supervisors, FTOs, and evaluators) opens the skills boards — see the skills article.
