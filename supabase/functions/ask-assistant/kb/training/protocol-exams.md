Protocol exams are proctored, timed online tests used at credentialing steps (for example P1C → P1). You don't sign up for one — Clinical Development assigns it to you.

**Taking one:**

1. Open [My Progress](/clinical-development) — your assigned exam shows as a card with **Begin exam** (or **Resume exam**).
2. The exam is proctored: it stays locked ("Your proctor will release this examination when you're seated and ready") until your proctor releases it — the page unlocks by itself.
3. Read the header (questions, time limit, passing score), then **Begin examination**. The clock starts and can't be paused.
4. Answer one question per screen — Previous/Next, a question grid to jump around, and some "select all that apply" or image questions. Answers autosave every couple of seconds.
5. **Review & submit** lists anything unanswered (unanswered scores as incorrect), then **Submit examination**. It also auto-submits at 0:00.

**Results** show immediately: your score and Passed / Not passed. A pass automatically checks your protocol-exam gate and files a completion certificate to your documents. The review screen shows which questions you missed with the protocol reference to study — correct answers are never revealed.

If you don't pass, Clinical Development will arrange the retake. Questions about an assignment go to the clinical staff ([Admin Staff](/admin-staff)).
