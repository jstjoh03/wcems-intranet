[Required Training](/training/required) lists compliance modules assigned to you by the Chief and Medical Director, split into **Outstanding** and **Completed**, each with its due date and status (Not started / In progress / Overdue). A dashboard banner nags while anything is outstanding.

**Completing a video module:**

1. Open the module and press play.
2. Watch it through — **skipping ahead is disabled**: scrubbing forward snaps you back to your furthest point (rewinding is fine). The progress bar shows "Watch progress N%".
3. When the video ends, the **Attestation of Completion** unlocks: read the attestation points, sign the signature pad, and tap **Submit attestation**.
4. Your certificate PDF downloads automatically, and you can re-download it from the module page any time.

**External-course modules** ("External course") unlock the attestation when you tap **Open the course** — complete the course on the external site, come back, sign, and submit.

Worth knowing:

- Watch progress follows you across devices — start on one device and the video resumes at your furthest point on another, and a video watched to the end unlocks the attestation everywhere (so you can watch on a laptop and sign on your phone).
- After you've completed a module you can re-watch and scrub freely.
- Who gets assigned what is set by admins (by role/shift/employment type, or tied to a scheduled event — event crews get push reminders).
- If an admin marked you complete (e.g., you attended a live session), it shows in Completed without a video step.

Your completion record (timestamp + signature) is the compliance proof; the certificate is your copy.
