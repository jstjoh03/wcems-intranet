Open [Protocols](/protocols) → the **Tools** tab at the bottom. Each tool opens as a pop-up sheet (close with ✕):

Clinical calculators:

- **STEMI Reference** — heart diagram, 12-lead placement, STEMI criteria, and Sgarbossa (original + modified).
- **Stroke Assessment** — Cincinnati (CPSS) and the VAN large-vessel-occlusion screen.
- **Burn Calculator** — Rule of Nines and a TBSA calculator with fluid rate by age.

OB / neonatal:

- **Contraction Tracker** — tap to time contractions (duration + frequency), export the log as PDF.
- **Delivery Documentation** — birth time, APGAR at 1 & 5 minutes, complications, exportable delivery report.
- **Newborn APGAR** — APGAR scoring with due-time reminders and PDF export.

(The delivery and APGAR tools share their data, so you don't enter the birth time twice. OB tool data lives only in that tab — it clears on reload, so export the PDF before closing.)

Reference sheets: **RASS Scale**, **H's and T's**, **Wong-Baker Pain Scale**, **Atropine Dosing** (organophosphate/SLUDGE), and the **SAM Splint User Guide**.

These tools support documentation and recall — they are not medical direction.
