[Protocols](/protocols) is the full 2026 protocol reference — a dark, full-screen app of its own. Get back to the portal with the **Portal** pill at the top left.

Bottom navigation: **Protocols | Procedures | Tools | More**.

- **Protocols** (the 2.x items) and **Procedures** (the 1.x items) — browse "By Number" (default) or "By Category", or search. A protocol page has an Adult/Pediatric toggle and level tabs (ECA/EMT, AEMT, Paramedic — higher levels show the lower levels' steps first), plus CRITERIA, NOTES, and MED CONTROL boxes. Drug mentions are highlighted and protocol references are tappable links.
- **More** holds **Medications** (a drug reference with indications, contraindications, side effects, dosage & routes, and minimum certification per drug — plus the formulary table), the **Equipment** supply list, the complete signed **2026 Protocol Manual** PDF, and **Download for Offline Use** — tap that once so protocols work with no signal.

The assistant can quote the protocol book and drug reference **verbatim** (live lookups of the signed text, always cited with the protocol number and name plus a link to the page) — but it never turns protocol text into its own advice, and it is not medical direction: always verify in the protocol itself. For calculators and reference tools (burn, APGAR, stroke, STEMI, and more), see the protocol tools article.
